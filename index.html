A LITTLE THOUGHT

THE DAY I PRAY FOR

Nikkah

Insha'Allah. Sarauniya, before I could call you my wife, you were already reminding me to eat, telling me to sleep, and checking if I was okay. Sometimes I think about how those little acts of care might feel when we're finally sharing the same home. I don't know when that day will come. Only Allah knows what He has written. But I pray that if this is our path, He guides us to it with patience, protects what is good between us, and places barakah in the home we hope to build. I want to keep becoming someone who can care for you with the same tenderness you have shown me. My Hayati, may Allah make our waiting meaningful, and our future full of peace.
